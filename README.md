# TRNT AI & Innovation Lab: Workshop Ideas

Workshop ideas for TRNT Educational Institute, where students aged 8–18 learn AI by building real things.

**Start here:** https://xappert.github.io/trnt-ai-lab/

**Bhavesh Ratanpal** · [bhaveshratanpal.ca](https://www.bhaveshratanpal.ca) · [xappert.ca](https://www.xappert.ca)

## Why I'm a good fit

- **I build AI products.** As founder of Xappert Incorporated, I lead AI product development, including XappertOS, a platform that helps founders turn ideas into ventures. That is the same entrepreneurial thinking TRNT wants students to learn.
- **I use what I teach.** I run my own voice-activated AI agent ([how I built it](https://xappert.github.io/trnt-ai-lab/jarvis/#mine)). I built this site with AI tools too, the same way students would.
- **I start from the user.** Every workshop here begins with a real person and a real problem, because that is how good products, and good learning, start.
- **I can explain technology clearly.** My work includes corporate training and writing. The pages here adapt each idea for ages 8–10, 11–12, and 13–18.
- **I contribute content, not just lessons.** This repository is a working start: a featured workshop with a first-session plan, ten workshop ideas, an incubator proposal with an interactive scorecard, and a parent note.

## The workshop I'd lead: Build Your Own Jarvis

Inspired by the AI assistant from the Iron Man films. I run a real Jarvis on my Mac mini that wakes up when I say "Hey Jarvis." In this workshop, students meet mine live, then design and build their own personal AI agent, with a version for every age from 8 to 18. [See the workshop](https://xappert.github.io/trnt-ai-lab/jarvis/).
