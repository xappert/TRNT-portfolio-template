# My Portfolio Website

A starter website you can make your own and publish on the internet in about 15 minutes. No installs, just a web browser.

**Live example:** https://xappert.github.io/TRNT-portfolio-template/

> **Instructors:** the project concepts are at https://xappert.github.io/TRNT-portfolio-template/concepts/ and the first-session plan is at https://xappert.github.io/TRNT-portfolio-template/workshop/
> **Students:** you can ignore the `concepts` and `workshop` folders. Only `index.html` is your page.

---

## Before you start

- You need a GitHub account (ages 13+, created with a parent's OK).
- **Stay safe online:** your site is public. Use your **first name only**. Do not add your last name, school name, photos of yourself, address, phone number or email.

---

## Step 1: Make your own copy

1. At the top of this page, click the green **Use this template** button, then **Create a new repository**.
2. Name it `portfolio`.
3. Keep it **Public**, then click **Create repository**.

## Step 2: Edit your page

1. In your new repository, click **index.html**.
2. Click the ✏️ pencil icon (top right) to edit.
3. Look for the lines that start with `✏️ STEP` and change the text below each one:

| Step | What to change |
|---|---|
| 1 | The page title (your first name) |
| 2 | Your colours |
| 3 | Your emoji |
| 4 | Your name and one-line intro |
| 5 | About me |
| 6 | Your interests |
| 7 | Something you're proud of |

4. Only change the words **between** the tags. For example, in `<h1>Hi, I'm Alex</h1>`, change `Alex` but leave `<h1>` and `</h1>` alone.
5. Click **Commit changes**, then **Commit changes** again.

## Step 3: Use AI as a co-writer

Stuck on "About me"? Ask an AI tool something like:

> Write 3 short sentences for a student portfolio. I'm in Grade 9, I like soccer and coding, and I want to be a game designer. Keep it simple and friendly.

Then **edit what it gives you**. Make it sound like you, and remove anything that isn't true.

## Step 4: Put it on the internet

1. In your repository, click **Settings**, then **Pages** (left menu).
2. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. Wait about 1 minute and refresh. Your link appears at the top:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/portfolio/`

🎉 Share your link with the group.

---

## Something broke?

- **Page looks plain or messy:** you probably deleted a `<` or `>`. Click **History**, open your last change, and compare.
- **Link shows "404":** wait another minute, then refresh. Check that Pages is set to `main`.
- **Changes don't show:** refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).

## What's next

Each workshop adds a new project to the **My projects** section, like an AI helper you design for someone you know or a smart gadget that uses a model you train yourself. By the end, you'll have a portfolio you can include in university and program applications.

Already built an AI helper? Its builder gives you a ready-made project card. Paste it into **My projects**, replacing one of the 🔜 cards.
