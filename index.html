The workshop I'd lead first

🦾 Build Your Own Jarvis

Many kids have dreamed of having an AI assistant like the one in the Iron Man films. I built a real one: it runs on my Mac mini at home and wakes up when I say "Hey Jarvis." In this workshop, students meet mine live, then design and build their own.

Ages 8–12Design their Jarvis: its name, personality, voice, and rules. Then watch it come to life on the big screen.
Ages 13–15Guided build: write its personality file and teach it a first skill.
Ages 16–18Full build: their own agent with memory, skills, a schedule, and a wake word.

📷Ages 8–18

Invent a Smart Gadget

Students train their own image, sound, or pose model and turn it into an invention: a recycling sorter, a posture coach, a sign-language reader.

They learn: how AI learns from data, why it fails, and how to make it fairer.

About these ideas

I build AI products for a living. At Xappert, our product work starts from what users need and then tests whether the solution actually works for them, and I run my own Jarvis, a voice-activated AI agent built on Hermes, day to day. These workshops give students the same process at their level: find a real person, solve a real problem, test it, and explain it.

The concepts are a starting point for discussion. Once we agree on a direction, I'm happy to shape the teaching format, session length, and materials around it.
