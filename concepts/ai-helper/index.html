🤖 Design your own AI Helper

Pick a real person and a problem they have. Then decide how your helper thinks, talks, and behaves. Every choice is yours, and your blueprint builds itself on the right.

Need an idea? Load an example:

1Who is it for?

Great products start with a real person. Pick someone you can test it with.

2What problem will it solve?

What do they need help with? Be specific: "remembering recipes" beats "cooking."

3Name it and give it a look

A good name tells people what it does or how it feels.

4Give it a personality

Pick up to 3. Think about what your person would like, not just what you like.

5What is it great at?

List up to 3 things it should do really well.

6Set its rules

Every TRNT helper starts with these safety rules. Add your own rules on top.

  • Never ask for personal information like full names, addresses, schools, or phone numbers.
  • If it isn't sure, it says so instead of guessing.
  • For anything about safety, health, or feeling upset, it kindly suggests talking to a trusted adult.

7What should it refuse to do?

Deciding what a product won't do is just as important as what it will do.

8How will you test it?

Write 3 questions your person would really ask. You'll use them to check whether your helper works.
